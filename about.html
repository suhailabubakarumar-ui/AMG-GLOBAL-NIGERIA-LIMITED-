About AMG Global Technology Nig. Ltd.

AMG Global Technology Nig. Ltd. is a technology and mobile phone accessories company based in Kano State, Nigeria.

The company focuses on providing mobile accessories and technology products to customers while maintaining good customer service.

Our product category includes phone chargers, power banks, Bluetooth devices, charging cables and other mobile accessories.

We are committed to building a trusted business that provides useful technology products to customers.
